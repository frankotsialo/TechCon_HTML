History of TechCon

TechCon began over a decade ago with a mission to bring together innovators, developers, and leaders in technology. Over the years, it has grown into a global conference, showcasing groundbreaking advancements and connecting thousands of professionals worldwide.

Our Mission

TechCon 2024 is committed to fostering collaboration, encouraging innovation, and inspiring the next generation of tech leaders. Our goal is to create an inclusive space where creativity and technology intersect to solve real-world problems.

Notable Past Speakers

Over the years, TechCon has welcomed inspiring voices from across the tech industry:

Dr. Ada Lovelace

Known as the first computer programmer, Dr. Lovelace inspired countless discussions about the role of creativity in programming and computational thinking.

Alan Turing

Pioneer in artificial intelligence, his talks at TechCon emphasized the future of machine learning and ethical AI.

Grace Hopper

Renowned computer scientist and U.S. Navy rear admiral, she inspired attendees with her groundbreaking work on programming languages.
